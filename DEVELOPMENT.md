## What it is

SC Companion helps you **craft in Star Citizen**. Everything starts from a simple question: *what do I want to craft, and what am I missing?* The app answers with the materials you need, where to mine them, where to buy what can't be crafted, and a ship configurator that sends a complete setup to crafting.

- **Always up to date**: the data comes from your installed game, so the app follows every patch.
- **Safe**: the game is never modified.
- **Free** for personal use.

## Features

### Crafting
- Pick Ship, FPS gear or Mission items, then narrow the list with filters (category, class, grade, size, weight, piece; type and family for ship weapons), or search by name, in English or French.
- For each blueprint: materials, quantities, minimum quality, what you already have in stock.
- Quality simulator: see the effect of material quality on the item before crafting.
- Which missions give a blueprint (click a mission for its details), and which blueprints you already own.

### Craft queue and cart
- Your projects grouped by ship or setup, with the shopping list of materials.
- Items that can't be crafted go to a cart that tells you where to buy them, with prices and a route.

### Mining
- **Mining route**: the fewest stops to get what you are missing.
- **Live signature scanner**: the app reads the number shown by the mining scanner and tells you which rock it is, in the app or in an overlay on top of the game. Rocks with an ore you need are marked ★.
- **Mining ships** (Prospector, MOLE, Golem, Hyun): heads and modules with their stats, and a rock simulator showing the effect of your setup and gadgets on a random rock.
- Guides for ores, locations and refineries.

### Inventory and refining
- By hand or from screenshots of the freight manager and the refinery.
- Your refining orders, with the time left.

### Ships
- Configurator: components, crafting qualities, power management, damage, defence, flight and quantum stats.
- Weapons: DPS, alpha, damage per projectile, penetration, range, and whether they get through a target ship's armor.
- Salvage: heads, modules and storage; your own salvage measurements, easy to share with the community; an operation calculator (RMC and aUEC per hour, solo or with a hauler).
- Your hangar of saved setups, sent to crafting in one click.

### Also
- Your owned blueprints ticked automatically while you play.
- Sync between your computers.
- English or French interface.
- Notice when a new version is out.
- Built-in guide (? or F1).

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or 11, Star Citizen installed on the same PC (recommended).
- For the live scanner and its overlay: the game in **Borderless** display mode.
